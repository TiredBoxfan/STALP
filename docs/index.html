STALP

What is STALP?

STALP (the Solution To All Life's Problems) is a thought experiment exploring a unified, from scratch redesign of conventions across many areas such as mathematical notation, units of measure, and computer programming, all under the notion of "getting it right this time."

DISCLAIMER: STALP is just a thought experiment and little more. While the amount of thought I have dedicated has caused some to accuse me of being more earnest than I claim, I assure you that I am under no illusion that we should throw out the accumulation of thousands of years of human conventions just because I think I can do better. The passion is there, just not the hubris.
